Draft copy; the CTA treatments above are visual examples. “Our recommended route” expresses Watch Reserve’s preference. Fast and Digital involve bringing or delivering the watch to the office; Remote starts with the seller’s photos and watch details.

Lead with the practical Remote benefit: “Upload your photos. Get your valuation. We’ll handle the next steps.” Use the strongest border, background and CTA for Remote. Fast Sale prioritises a quick sale; Remote makes the initial valuation convenient. A quick valuation does not promise a quick completed sale.

03 / One form or three?

Approach Visitor experience Planning assessment
One adaptable form — proposed starting point All three CTAs land at the form. The chosen route is visible, editable and included in the enquiry. The existing core questions apply to all three routes. Reuse the form and tailor its heading, short explanation and any genuinely necessary conditional questions.
Three separate forms Each route has its own questions and submission journey. Consider only if eligibility, required information or operations differ enough to justify separate experiences. Shared components can still prevent duplicate validation and upload code.

The proposal keeps one adaptable form and the existing three steps. For Remote, make the photo step central: explain that the valuation is based on the submitted images and details, and propose requiring usable photos before submission. Keep photos optional for Fast and Digital enquiries, where the watch is brought or delivered to the office. These conditional requirements are proposed; photos are currently optional for every submission.

04 / Make the landing feel connected

  • Carry the selected route in sale_type: fast, digital or remote. Use the existing #start anchor and account for the sticky header.
  • Show “Your choice: Remote Sale” and “Get your remote watch valuation”. Explain: “Upload clear photos and your watch details. Our team will review them and contact you with your valuation and next steps.” Keep the option editable without losing entered details.
  • For direct visitors, allow a route choice or “Help me choose”. Do not infer Remote from a missing or invalid URL value.
  • A valid explicit CTA choice should take precedence over a saved choice. Preserve the watch/contact draft, and show any resumed step clearly. Test both fresh and resumed visits.
  • Update the current /sell copy, which describes only Consignment and Outright Sale. Its success message also currently says “Consignment Page Valuation Form”.
  • The existing Ask Me Later reminder can appear on /sell. Decide whether to suppress it for this focused funnel visit so it does not compete with the main form.

05 / Capture the choice and measure completion

Proposed additions: validate the final sale type on the server; include it in the internal notification, customer confirmation and Klaviyo profile metadata. Keep entry source and sale type separate, and retain the initial choice if the visitor changes route.

Proposed event When Useful properties
sale_options_view Homepage section becomes visible Section version
sale_option_select A card CTA is clicked Initial sale type, entry source
sell_form_start First meaningful form interaction Sale type, fresh/resumed
sell_form_step_complete A step passes validation Step, sale type
sell_form_submit_success API confirms submission Initial and final sale type, source

Use the existing consent-aware analytics helper; do not send names, emails, phone numbers or watch photos to analytics. Compare completed enquiries per section viewer and per form starter, plus the Remote share of completed enquiries. Treat click share as supporting evidence. Consent-based analytics will cover only part of the audience; assess enquiry quality and eventual sales separately through internal reporting.

06 / Decisions needed before implementation

  1. After the remote valuation: confirm when physical inspection or delivery is needed, who handles it, and how listing approval follows. The initial valuation uses seller-submitted photos and details; later custody arrangements remain to be defined.
  2. Fast Sale: is this the current outright-purchase service? Confirm eligibility, offer/payment timing and any genuine price-versus-speed trade-off.
  3. Commercial terms: agree fees, seller responsibilities and the stage at which listing permission is obtained for Digital and Remote.
  4. Photo requirements: agree the minimum usable images and the process for requesting clearer photos or missing details. Proposed guidance: clear views of the dial, case/back and condition, within the existing four-image upload limit. Revalidate Remote requirements if a visitor changes route on a later step.
