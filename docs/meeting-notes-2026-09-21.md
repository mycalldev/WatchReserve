### Remote sale funnel objective

- **Conor:** The objective is to make the initial connection with clients and collect the details of watches they are prepared to sell.
- The **Remote Sale** is intended to act as a catchy lead magnet that captures the client’s initial contact details.
- Once contact has been made, Watch Reserve can arrange photography and collect any other information or materials required for the sale.
- The funnel is intended to capture leads and build a database of watches available for sale.
- **Positioning:** “Remote Sale” is the Watch Reserve lead magnet and is required to capture leads.
- **Marketing terminology:** The Remote Sale is a **lead magnet** within a **lead-generation funnel**; because it acquires sellers and their watches, it can also be described as a **seller acquisition funnel**.

### Brand positioning and trust concern

- **Conor:** Raising a concern that if Watch Reserve appears to offer a service to buy watches through a Remote Sale, website visitors could infer that Watch Reserve is involved in selling fake watches.
- The underlying issue is **category confusion**: visitors may interpret Watch Reserve as a direct buyer or reseller rather than as a service that helps clients sell their own watches.
- The communication concepts being considered are **strategic ambiguity**, **reframing**, **non-engagement**, and **brand/category distancing**.
- A safer positioning approach is to define the role clearly and pre-empt the misunderstanding, rather than relying on silence. The Remote Sale should be framed as a client-seller acquisition service, with the authentication, provenance, and collection process stated accurately wherever applicable.
- Useful analogy: **a clearly signposted consignment desk, not a silent or ambiguous marketplace**. The signposting separates Watch Reserve’s role from the source and authenticity of the watch.

### Sell your watch lead-capture interaction

- When a user clicks the **Sell your watch** link in the top bar, open a dedicated slide-in panel.
- The slide-in should be specifically focused on the **Sell your watch** / Remote Sale lead capture.
- Required fields:
  - Full Name
  - Email
